# Pricing & Plans

## ZenTable Pricing
ZenTable ke plans flexible hain aur restaurant ki zaroorat ke hisaab se customize kiye jaate hain. System abhi 3 global plans (**Basic, Pro, Elite**) aur custom add-ons support karta hai jinki pricing Admin panel ke "Subscription & Add-ons Manager" se manage ki jati hai.

---

## Free Trial
- **1 mahine ka free trial** — poore features ke saath
- Trial mein milta hai:
  - Full platform access
  - 5 pre-existing 3D models (AR menu ke liye)
  - Digital menu + QR codes
  - Ordering system (Customer direct ordering)
  - Analytics dashboard
  - Staff accounts (Owner, Waiter, Kitchen, Counter)
- Koi credit card nahi chahiye trial ke liye
- Trial ke baad team discuss karegi aage ke plan (Basic, Pro, ya Elite) ke baare mein.

## Common Questions

**Q: Kya Basic plan mein staff panel aur ordering milti hai?**
A: Haan! Basic plan mein ab QR ordering, staff panel (waiter/kitchen), aur basic POS shamil hain.

**Q: AR menu aur Kitchen Tab ke liye alag charge hoga?**
A: Haan, AR menu aur Kitchen Tab **Add-ons** hain. Aap inhe kisi bhi base plan ke upar add kar sakte hain.

**Q: Multi-branch aur Delivery system kaunse plan mein aata hai?**
A: Yeh dono features **Pro** aur **Elite** plans mein available hain.

**Q: Contract kitne time ka hota hai?**
A: Flexible plans hain — monthly aur annual dono options discuss ho sakte hain.
